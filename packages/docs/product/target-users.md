# 3DSL Target Users v0.1

Status: `APPROVED / PRODUCT_DEFINITION_V1`

## Primary role: Reader / Explorer

A person trying to understand a subject whose structure is difficult to hold in one sequential view.

Typical need patterns:
- many related concepts or entities;
- whole/part or multiple-scale structure;
- several relation types;
- temporal or conditional structure;
- comparison across domains, periods or viewpoints;
- need to revisit context while reading details.

This definition is role-based, not profession-based.
Possible domains include law, philosophy, systems, science, engineering, mathematics and language, but the product is not limited to those categories.

### Reader job

> 「この項目は全体のどこにあり、周囲とどうつながり、内部では何が起きているか」を行き来しながら理解したい。

## Secondary role: Author / Mapper

A person who constructs a structural representation in order to:
- make distinctions explicit;
- position and relate elements;
- compare candidate structures;
- inspect inconsistencies or missing connections;
- communicate the resulting structure to readers.

### Author job

> 複雑な対象を配置・接続しながら、自分自身の理解を更新し、他者が辿れる形にする。

## Later role: Collaborative curator

A person or group connecting multiple models, interpretations or disciplines into a shared atlas.

This is a later-stage user role, not a first-release requirement.

## Explicit non-target for first release

The first release is not optimized for:
- generic 3D asset creation;
- entertainment 3D scenes;
- CAD;
- real-time multi-user editing;
- universal ontology engineering.

## Role priority

```text
1. Reader / Explorer
2. Author / Mapper
3. Collaborative curator (later)
```

This ordering follows the Library/Viewer-first product roadmap and keeps Modeler from becoming the public-release blocker.
